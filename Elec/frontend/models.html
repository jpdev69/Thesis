Model Insight
Comparing multiple models helps identify which approach works best for your data.
Baseline Methods (Evaluated on identical 80/20 Holdout Test Window):
  • Persistence (Naive): Predicts next value = current value (simplest benchmark)
  • Historical Mean: Predicts average of training period
  • Linear Regression: Fits linear trend on training split, evaluated on test split
  • ARIMA: Classical ARIMA benchmark trained on 80% split, evaluated on 20% holdout
  • Attention-LSTM + RBF-SVR: Active hybrid model trained on 80% split, evaluated on 20% holdout
